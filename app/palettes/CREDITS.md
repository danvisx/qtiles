# Palette credits

## Authored for qtiles

qtiles, Earth and stone, Foliage and water, Dusk (cool), Warm morning, Monochrome 8, Skin and hair. qtiles and Monochrome 8 are hand-picked colors; the others are generated by `makeRamp` in `app/js/palettes.js` from base colors.
