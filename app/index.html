Outline

Tap to place one at the size above, or drag to draw its footprint. It lands on the surface under the cursor: the ground, a flat top, or a slope at its height there, on any layer that is showing. Roofs go on a box of the same size. Hold a label on touch to read what it does. Width and depth are in half tiles. A window, door, sign or awning goes on the wall you tap, a chimney on a top or a roof, and each brings its own colors.

Scatters
Options
Seed

Drag to paint. The same stroke and seed give the same result. Reroll redoes your last stroke with a new seed.

Removes what the stroke touches on the active layer. An assembly goes as a whole. Terrain stays.

Dragging moves

Tap an object to select it, or drag across empty canvas to select everything you touch. Shift-tap or Add to selection adds and removes. Dragged objects land on the surface under them and snap to whatever stands beside them; Alt or Free turns snapping off, leaving half tiles and single pixels of height. A detail slides along its wall. Arrow keys move one tile, Page Up and Down one level, End lands, Delete removes.

Sides

Drag over tiles. Each tile changes once per stroke, up to 8 levels. Level sets every tile to the height you started on. The top takes the current color. It paints on a Terrain layer, made at the bottom if there is none.

Dragging

Tap the point between the character's feet. Then Place on tile, under Character, keeps it on tile centers as the layer moves. An object layer moves with Select object instead.

Drag to select. Drag inside a selection to move it. Tap outside to deselect.

Wand reads

Tap to select contiguous pixels of exactly the same color.

Each pixel moves one step along its palette ramp per stroke. Colors outside a ramp are skipped.

Edge

Drag out from the center of the footprint. It goes on this layer's shadow layer, which is created if there is none.

Rim

Tap to stamp one tile, or drag to fill a rectangle of tiles. Textures use the first color's ramp. The same tile and seed always get the same variant.

Other pixels

Tap the second swatch or press X to swap the two colors.

Dither in at most two colors that are neighbors on the ramp; avoid dithering across large jumps.

Use dither on large flat areas, not on small details.

Facings

Down-right and down-left are mirrors, and so are up-right and up-left. A mirror keeps its colors, but a held item changes hands, so check it. Update mirror replaces hand fixes on the mirror.

The ghost shows faintly over everything to trace. It is never exported, and it cannot be picked or drawn on until you clear it.
